🌿 Neem-Based Organic Fertilizers

Neem (Azadirachta indica) has powerful natural properties that make it an excellent fertilizer and natural pesticide.


🧪 1. Neem Cake Fertilizer

Neem Cake is the solid residue left after extracting oil from neem seeds.

✅ Benefits:

Rich in N-P-K (Nitrogen, Phosphorus, Potassium) Natural pest and nematode repellent Enhances soil fertility Acts as a slow-release fertilizer
🛠 How to Prepare Neem Cake at Home: Note: If you don't have access to a neem oil press, use neem seeds or neem seed powder.

Ingredients:

Neem seeds (sun-dried) OR neem seed powder – 1 kg Optional: cow dung or compost – 1 kg (for added nutrients) Steps: Dry neem seeds in sunlight for 2–3 days. Grind the seeds into a coarse powder. Mix with compost or cow dung. Let it rest for 5–7 days before applying.
Usage: Soil Application: 100–200 g per plant or 200–500 kg per acre. Mix into soil at planting time or as top dressing.

🌿 2. Neem Oil Emulsion (Liquid Pesticide + Fertilizer)

While primarily a pesticide, neem oil also provides micronutrients.

🛠 Ingredients:

        Neem oil – 30 ml                                                                               .

        Water – 1 liter

        Mild soap – 5 ml (for emulsifying)
🧪 Steps:
Mix soap and neem oil to create an emulsion.
Slowly add this to 1 liter of water. Stir thoroughly.
Use: Spray on leaves and soil every 7–15 days.

🌱 3. Neem Leaf Compost

Ingredients:

Neem leaves – 2–5 kg (fresh or dry) Other composting materials (veg peels, cow dung, straw)
Steps: 
        Layer neem leaves with other organic matter.                                                    

        Add cow dung as an accelerator.

        Turn pile every 10 days.

        Ready in 40–60 days.
Use: Apply as compost to any garden or field crop.

🌿 4. Neem Seed Kernel Extract (NSKE)

An excellent bio-pesticide and soil stimulant.

Ingredients:

Neem seed kernels – 500 g Water – 10 liters 
        Steps:
        Pound or grind kernels into powder.                                                             

        Soak in water overnight.

        Filter using cloth.

        Spray immediately (add mild soap if needed).
Use: Spray on crops to prevent pests and nourish plants.

🐄 Dung-based organic Fertilizer & Pesticide


🛠️ 1. How to Prepare Cow Dung Manure (Composted)

🧴 Method:

Simple Composting
Ingredients:
                Fresh cow dung
                Dry leaves or straw (carbon-rich)
                Water
                A pit or bin
Steps:
Mix cow dung with dry matter (1:1 ratio). Stack in a pile or bin. Water lightly every few days. Turn the pile every 10–15 days. Ready in 45–60 days when dark and crumbly with no smell.
Use:
1–2 handfuls per pot
2–3 tons/acre for farms (as basal dose)

💧 2. Cow Dung Slurry (Liquid Fertilizer)

Ingredients:

1 kg cow dung                                                                                           
                    10 liters of water
Steps:
Mix cow dung in water to form a thin slurry.
Ferment for 2–3 days in shade.
Stir daily.
Filter and apply near root zone or via drip.
Use: Every 15 days for vegetables, grains, and flowers.

🌿 3. Cow Dung + Urine: Jeevamrut (Highly Effective)

Ingredients:

10 kg cow dung
10 liters cow urine
2 kg jaggery
2 kg gram flour
Handful of native soil
200 liters of water
How to Use: Ferment for 5–7 days (stir daily)
Apply to soil or spray on leaves every 10–15 days
